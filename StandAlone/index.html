About the food

Fresh ingredients. Killer comfort food.

Zaviya Grill serves Pakistani, Indian and Punjabi dishes in North Austin. Grills, curries, biryani, breads, desserts and drinks, made with house-ground spices, halal meat and fresh vegetables.

A different angle

Zaviya means a unique perspective

Chef and restaurateur Kashif Taj opened Mirchi Restaurant on Parmer Lane in 2014. Zaviya Grill grew from it. In 2019 he partnered with physician Junaid Siddiqui, M.D., who wanted similar foods made with healthier ingredient swaps.

  • House-ground spices
  • Halal meat
  • Fresh vegetables, prepared daily
